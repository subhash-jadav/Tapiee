Tapiee

Make someone a mixtape: songs, a cassette you design, one link.

Design systemBrand book

Tapiee turns YouTube songs into a cassette mixtape that someone designs and sends with one link. Everything should feel like a handmade tape from the 90s: warm paper, chunky ink outlines, hard printed shadows, a little bounce. Build every screen from these tokens, styles and components.

Voice and copy

Tapiee talks like a friend who still makes mixtapes: warm, short, a little playful, never corporate.

  • Write in sentence case. Use "you" for the reader; the maker signs off as "Shubh" ("Built with ♡ by Shubh", "— Shubh ♡").
  • Lean on tape vocabulary where it fits: side A / side B, "the case", "open the case", "TAPE CHEWED" (not found), "TAPE JAMMED" (crash), "reading your songs…".
  • Buttons say exactly what happens and may end with a glyph: "Start your mixtape", "Make a mixtape →", "Save & get link ✦", "↻ Try again", "Share this tape ↗".
  • Errors say what went wrong and what to do, with no blame and no apology: "YouTube didn't respond. Try again." · "The link might be mistyped, or the tape was deleted by the person who made it. Ask them to send the link again." · "Your saved tapes are safe."
  • Kicker labels are uppercase in the eyebrow style with middots: "STEP 1 · THE SONGS", "SOMEONE MADE YOU A MIXTAPE", "ERROR 404 · TAPE CHEWED".
  • Emoji only as functional icons (🎲 random palette, 🔊 sound, ✨ stickers tab), never as decoration in sentences.

Colour

  • Page ground is cream with the faint dot texture. Raised surfaces are paper. Inputs are field.
  • All text, borders and icons are ink; secondary text is ink-muted.
  • orange is the one accent: primary buttons, eyebrows, the logo's full stop, the focus ring. mustard is the second accent for highlights and cassette stripes.
  • cherry means error or remove. teal, denim and brown are for cassette art, stickers and illustrations, never for UI text (except denim, which reads at 6.79:1).
  • Two themes ship: Light (classic) and Dark. Every colour that changes between them is a token; never hard-code a light-only colour in a component.
  • Shared tapes always render in the creator's classic design, whatever theme the viewer uses (.theme-classic in the app).

Contrast. Ink on cream, paper and field is 12.6:1 or better in both themes. Two light-theme pairs from the source fall short and are kept as they are:

  • orange text on cream is 2.52:1. Use orange text only for large type (24px+) or short uppercase eyebrows, never for body copy.
  • on-orange on orange (primary buttons) is 2.90:1. Labels stay 16px semibold. ink on orange (5.22:1) is the passing alternative.

In dark, both pairs pass (7.4:1 and 7.3:1).

Type

All faces are Google Fonts, loaded by app/fonts.ts.

  • Shrikhand (display): titles and the wordmark. Use display-xl / display-lg on wide screens and display-md on phones. Never for body text or buttons.
  • Space Grotesk (body): everything you read and tap: body-lg for intros, body, button (600), small, caption, label.
  • Special Elite (type): typewriter kickers and fine print: eyebrow, meta. Always uppercase with 0.1em tracking.
  • Permanent Marker (hand): personal touches only, such as a signature or the note in the case.
  • VT323 (pixel): the player's LCD only.

Cassette and cover designs also offer these faces to tape makers: Caveat, Rock Salt, Gloria Hallelujah, Bungee, Pacifico, Playfair Display and Cormorant Garamond (italic). They belong to the tape art, not the app chrome.

Layout

  • Content sits in a centred column, max-width 1024px (64rem), with a space-4 side gutter.
  • Headers: the "Tapiee." wordmark on the left, round IconButtons on the right, space-4 vertical padding.
  • Group things with gap, not margins: space-2 inside controls, space-3 between buttons, space-6–space-8 between sections.
  • Phones first: everything works at 320px. Two-column layouts (Create page) collapse to one column below 768px, and both columns may shrink (minmax(0, 1fr)) so long song titles truncate instead of pushing the layout.
  • The footer is small and faded (60% opacity): "Built with ♡ by Shubh · v<version>" and "say hi on insta @subhashh.jadav ✌︎".

Shape and depth

  • Every interactive surface has a 2px ink border.
  • Corners: radius-lg (inputs, chips, small buttons), radius-xl (buttons), radius-2xl (cards, popovers), radius-full (icon buttons, swatches, player keys).
  • Shadows are hard and offset down-right with no blur: shadow-sm → shadow-btn → shadow-card → shadow-device. Never use blurred or coloured shadows.
  • Illustrations (cassettes, the spilled-tape error art) are flat inline SVG in token colours: no gradients except the player body and LCD.

Motion

See the Motion section for every animation. In short: things lift on hover with ease-spring, press down quickly with dur-press, and enter with a short fade-up. Everything respects reduced motion.

Accessibility

  • Keyboard focus: a solid 2px orange outline, 3px offset, on every button, link and input. Dark: 7.4:1 against cream. Light: 2.52:1, below the 3:1 focus-ring target; flagged.
  • Inputs also glow with focus-glow while focused.
  • Touch targets are at least 32px (icon buttons) and 40px for buttons.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce shortens every animation and transition to near zero.
  • Never rely on colour alone: errors include words, states include text or glyphs.

YouTube

Songs play through YouTube's embedded player. Show YouTube as the source near the player, don't hide the player or play audio-only, and follow the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Ambient

  • Cassette reels spin (reel-spin, 1.8s linear) only while music plays, and spin up and down like a motor.
  • The hero cassette floats gently (float, 6s).
  • The LCD title scrolls (marquee, 12s) when it is too long to fit.
  • Equaliser bars bounce (eq) during playback.

Transitions between states

  • Theme change: the new theme spreads out as a circle from the pressed button (View Transitions).
  • Reordering songs: rows glide to their new places (FLIP). While dragging with ⠿, neighbours slide aside on 160ms ease.
  • Removing a row: it fades and collapses its height.
  • The editor tab indicator slides under the active tab, 360ms ease-spring.

Reduced motion

With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, every animation and transition drops to 0.01ms and plays once. Nothing important may depend on an animation finishing.

LibraryComponents

Button

The chunky retro button: a 2px ink border, 12px corners and a hard offset shadow that lifts on hover and presses flat when clicked.

Classes: btn plus one fill, optionally btn-sm.

VariantUse
btn-primaryThe one main action on a screen: "Start your mixtape", "Make a mixtape →", "↻ Try again". Fill orange, text on-orange.
btn-ghostSecondary actions next to a primary one: "Go home", "Share this tape ↗". Fill paper.
btn-darkStrong actions inside panels: "Save & get link ✦", "Copy". Fill ink, text paper.
btn-smCompact header buttons: "Make one", "↗ Share". Radius radius-lg, shadow-sm.

The consumer provides the label (sentence case, may end with an arrow or glyph: → ↗ ↻ ✦) and the click handler. Disabled buttons fade to 50% and ignore taps.

Do

  • Use one btn-primary per view.
  • Say exactly what happens: "Save & get link", not "Submit".
  • Keep the motion: hover lifts 1px with shadow-btn-hover, press pushes 2px with shadow-btn-press over dur-press.

Don't

  • Don't add blur shadows or gradients: Tapiee's shadows are hard and flat.
  • Light theme: on-orange on orange is 2.90:1. Keep primary labels at 16px semibold or larger; consider ink text if contrast must pass AA.

A 32px round button with a single glyph, used in the site header for About (ⓘ), theme (☾ / ☀), random palette (🎲) and sound (🔊 / 🔇).

Field

YouTube didn't respond. Try again.

A text input with its label, used for tape titles, the note, and the song search box.

  • Label: the label text style, uppercase, ink-muted, 4px above the input.
  • Input: field fill, 2px ink border, radius-lg, 8px × 12px padding, body text.
  • Focus adds focus-glow (a soft orange ring) on ease-out, plus the global keyboard focus ring.
  • Errors sit right under the field in cherry at 14px: say what went wrong and what to do ("YouTube didn't respond. Try again."). Keep the typed text.

The consumer provides the label text, the value, the placeholder (an example, not instructions), and any error message.

Chip

A small toggle button for picking one option from a short set: cover patterns, fonts, scenes in the editor.

  • Off: transparent with a faint ink border (20%). On (data-on="true"): filled ink with paper text.
  • Press shrinks it to 94% over dur-press.
  • Use a row of chips for 2–8 options. More than that, use a list.

The consumer provides the option labels and which one is on (also set aria-pressed).

Swatch

A 28px round colour button for cassette and cover colours in the editor.

  • Selected (data-on="true"): ink border, 110% scale and a double ring (paper then ink) so the choice reads on any colour.
  • Hover grows it to 112% on ease-spring.
  • Always give each swatch an aria-label with the colour's name.

The consumer provides the colour, its name, and the selected state.

Toast

Saved ✓ Added 4 songs · 1 already picked Couldn't save. Try again.

A short message that slides up at the bottom centre after an action, then leaves on its own.

  • Success: ink fill, paper text, shadow-btn. Error: cherry fill.
  • Enters with toast-in (slide up 14px, ease-spring), leaves with toast-out.
  • Keep it to one line. State the result in past tense ("Saved ✓", "Copied!"), or what failed and what to do.
  • An optional action (Undo) is an underlined text button inside the toast.

The consumer provides the message, success or error, and an optional action.

Tabs

The editor's six-panel switcher (Songs, Tape, Cover, Inside, Stickers, Share), each tab an icon over a word: ♫ 📼 🖼 💌 ✨ 🔗.

  • A solid ink indicator slides under the active tab on ease-spring (360ms); the active label turns paper.
  • Mark the active tab with aria-current="page".
  • Switching panels plays panel-in (fade up 8px).

The consumer provides the tabs (glyph + word) and the active one. Keep it to six or fewer so labels fit on a 320px phone.

The walkman-style music player on shared tapes: a body, an LCD screen, hardware-style buttons, and a side A/B switch.

  • Light body: gradient device-top → device-bottom, shadow-device. Dark body (device-dark) for dark backdrops.
  • LCD: green (lcd, lcd-ink) on light; amber (lcd-amber) glowing on dark, with faint scanlines. Text uses the lcd style (VT323).
  • Hardware buttons sit on a 4px solid shadow and travel down 3px when pressed over dur-press. Play is the orange hw-btn-primary.
  • The LCD shows side and track ("SIDE A · 01/08"), the title (scrolls when long) and the artist.
  • iPhone/iPad show a mute button instead of the volume slider, because iOS ignores web volume changes.

The consumer provides the tracks, the current side and track, and play state. The YouTube video plays inside the player (see the compliance note in the README).
